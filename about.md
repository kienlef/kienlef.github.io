# Frank Kienle helps connect analytics, AI, and operations with business context.

<p class="kf-page-lead">Frank works at the intersection of practical analytics, AI, and operations. His public material helps professionals, students, and teams understand how data, automation, and agentic AI can create operational value without losing process ownership or responsible review.</p>

## Background

Frank is a former university lecturer and an industry digital and analytics leader. His public work combines educational explanations, practical frameworks, and GitHub-based artifacts so readers can inspect the examples instead of relying on unsupported claims.
