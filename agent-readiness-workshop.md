# Put the workflow, evidence, ownership, and blockers on the table before you build an agent.

<p class="kf-page-lead">The Agent Readiness Workshop turns a text checklist into a 25–35 minute working session. Participants write one workflow, lay out its boundaries, sort the agent's authority, place evidence and blocker tokens, run a failure drill, and leave with one decision, one evidence action, and one named owner.</p>

<div class="kf-note"><strong>Version 0.2:</strong> 15 editable slides plus a six-page A4 landscape print kit. Examples are generic and synthetic. The score supports a decision; it does not certify a workflow or replace internal governance.</div>

## What participants physically do

<div class="kf-grid-3">
  <div class="kf-card"><div class="kf-icon"><i class="ph ph-note-pencil"></i></div><h3>Write one workflow</h3><p>Use a trigger, an operational task, and a real decision or handoff. “Use AI for operations” does not count.</p></div>
  <div class="kf-card"><div class="kf-icon"><i class="ph ph-path"></i></div><h3>Place its boundaries</h3><p>Lay string or arrows from trigger to stop. Mark the human gate and the exception route.</p></div>
  <div class="kf-card"><div class="kf-icon"><i class="ph ph-cards"></i></div><h3>Sort authority</h3><p>Place each step under observe, draft, recommend, execute, escalate, or never automate.</p></div>
  <div class="kf-card"><div class="kf-icon"><i class="ph ph-stamp"></i></div><h3>Name the owner</h3><p>Pass the decision-owner baton. Name who owns, approves, reviews, and can stop the workflow.</p></div>
  <div class="kf-card"><div class="kf-icon"><i class="ph ph-warning-octagon"></i></div><h3>Expose blockers</h3><p>Place blocker tokens where evidence, permission, failure visibility, or human review is missing.</p></div>
  <div class="kf-card"><div class="kf-icon"><i class="ph ph-signpost"></i></div><h3>Choose the next move</h3><p>Finish with one readiness label, one next evidence action, and one named owner.</p></div>
</div>

## The seven dimensions

The canvas covers the business problem, workflow boundary, data readiness, decision and action scope, human review, failure visibility, and governance/confidentiality. Each dimension receives a 0, 1, or 2 token only after the team places visible evidence.

<section class="kf-section dark">
  <span class="kf-eyebrow"><i class="ph ph-hand"></i> The workshop rule</span>
  <h2>No evidence means zero. A hard blocker can override an attractive total.</h2>
  <p>A low score is useful when it finds the missing process, data, ownership, or control work before an agent makes it expensive.</p>
</section>

## Use it responsibly

This material supports educational and professional discussion. It does not provide legal, financial, compliance, security, data-protection, works-council, or employer-specific advice. Adapt the workshop to internal policies and expert review. Use public, generic, synthetic, or specifically approved information; do not place confidential operational material in public examples.
