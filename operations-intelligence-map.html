Operations intelligence map

One operating system. Twenty-seven decisions. Six ways to see the connections.

The map is structured on the ASCM SCOR Digital Standard — Orchestrate, Plan, Order, Source, Transform, Fulfill, Return. Switch the lens to see the same decisions through standard processes, roles, systems, data, and AI roles. Select any node for the decision owner, the boundary, and the supporting material.

The boundaryThe map shows where an agent can predict, detect, classify, optimise, simulate, monitor, coordinate, or summarise. It does not hand the decision to the software.
