Operations intelligence map

One operating system. Sixteen decisions. Five ways to see the connections.

Use the lenses to explore how operational decisions connect through processes, people, systems, data, and AI. Click any use case to inspect the human owner and the supporting evidence.

The boundary: the map shows where an agent can predict, detect, optimise, simulate, monitor, or coordinate. It does not hand the decision to the software.
Switch lenses to redraw the relationships. Select a node for the operational detail.
